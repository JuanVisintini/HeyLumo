# 01 - Vision

# AI Business Platform

> Create a professional business website in minutes by automatically transforming existing online information into a complete digital presence.

---

# Vision

Small businesses should not have to hire a designer, developer, copywriter and SEO specialist just to have a professional website.

Our vision is to build an AI-powered platform capable of automatically creating and maintaining the complete digital presence of any business.

The user should only provide the minimum amount of information required—ideally a single social media profile or business listing—and the platform will do everything else.

---

# Mission

Digitize any local business in less than five minutes.

Not build websites.

Digitize businesses.

---

# The Problem

Today, creating a business website usually requires:

- Choosing a template.
- Designing pages.
- Writing content.
- Editing images.
- Configuring SEO.
- Purchasing hosting.
- Configuring a domain.
- Deploying the project.
- Maintaining the website.

For many local businesses this process is too expensive, too technical and too time-consuming.

As a result, many businesses rely only on Instagram or Facebook as their digital presence.

---

# Our Solution

Our platform automatically reconstructs a business's online presence.

Instead of asking users to manually build a website, we automatically collect available business information, organize it, enrich it with AI and generate a complete production-ready website.

The entire process should require as little interaction as possible.

Example:

Instagram URL

↓

Business Import

↓

AI Analysis

↓

Website Generation

↓

Deployment

↓

Website Online

---

# Product Principles

## Automation First

Never ask the user for information that can be discovered automatically.

---

## AI Should Be Invisible

The user should never need to write prompts.

AI works behind the scenes.

---

## One Click Experience

The platform should automate everything possible.

If a manual step is unavoidable (for example purchasing a custom domain), it should happen only after the website is already available.

---

## Production Ready Output

The platform generates real projects.

Not demos.

Every generated website must be deployable, maintainable and scalable.

---

## Consistency Over Creativity

The platform should use predefined design systems and reusable components instead of generating arbitrary HTML.

This guarantees quality, accessibility and performance.

---

# Initial Target Market

The MVP focuses on local businesses that usually have:

- Instagram
- Google Business Profile
- Photos
- Menus
- Opening hours

Examples:

- Restaurants
- Cafés
- Bars
- Bakeries

Future versions can support:

- Hotels
- Gyms
- Clinics
- Barber shops
- Retail stores
- Professional services

---

# Value Proposition

We are not building another website builder.

We are building an automatic business digitization platform.

Users should feel they are importing their business—not creating a website.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The website is only the first product.

Once business information has been imported and structured, the platform can generate additional digital assets such as:

- Landing pages
- QR menus
- NFC experiences
- Promotional campaigns
- Social media content
- Email campaigns
- AI assistants
- Booking systems
- Customer portals

The imported business data becomes the foundation for an entire ecosystem of AI-powered digital tools.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful first experience should look like this:

1. Paste Instagram URL.
2. Click "Generate".
3. Wait less than two minutes.
4. Receive a fully deployed website.

No templates.

No coding.

No configuration.

Only results.
